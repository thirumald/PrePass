      Use cases on the map

      Markers sit at illustrative sites near real hospitals, civil defence stations and roads. Each popup adds real data found near the point and, where one exists, opens the matching scripted scenario in Live.

      Corridor planner route · bumps · RapidPass run

      Pilot corridors

      Each major hospital is paired with its nearest civil defence station. Routes are ranked by the time speed bumps cost an emergency run, so the top row is the best place to pilot first.

      Response coverage

      Shaded areas can be reached from a civil defence station within 4, 6 and 8 minutes. White gaps are where a response takes longer.

      Drone docks

      Docking stations on traffic-signal poles let drones land, recharge and launch again, linking aerial views to the ground network. Sites are real signal poles from OpenStreetMap, chosen to cover hospitals, civil defence stations and today's jams and closures.

      Send a drone

      Privacy and flight register

      Who is watching, what is collected, how it is used. These rules are enforced before a drone launches, and every flight, including refused ones, is recorded here and in the Passes audit log.
      • Purpose limit: only incident response, congestion monitoring and emergency escort. Anything else is refused.
      • Blurring: faces and plates must be blurred on board. A flight without it is refused.
      • No-fly zones: flights into or across airport buffers are refused (shown in red on the map).
      • Retention: footage is deleted after the period below. This app records flight details only; it stores no video or personal data.
      • Transparency: the register below lists every flight, with who sent it and why.
      Aviation rules in the UAE are set by the GCAA. The zones here are illustrative buffers, not legal advice.

      Hospital access

      Speed bumps come from OpenStreetMap and may be incomplete. Counts are within 1 km, so they are an upper bound for any one approach.

      Layers

      Map: OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap contributors. Traffic, places and routing: TomTom. Routes without a key use the free OSRM demo server.

      Passes

      Signed priority passes for emergency vehicles. Dispatch status decides; the edge verifies signature, counter and status. Runs in your browser with WebCrypto, nothing is sent anywhere.
